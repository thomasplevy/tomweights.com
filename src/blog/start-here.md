This blog is where I write the long version of things I'd otherwise squeeze into a LinkedIn post and regret. It's mostly about how software, and the teams that build it, actually behave (as opposed to how the diagrams say they behave), with detours into AI tooling, WordPress, and whatever I'm skeptical of that month.

There isn't much here yet, so for now this page is less a curated reading list than a promissory note. If you only read one thing, make it this:

- [The Bill Was the Most Honest Document We Had](/blog/intended-vs-running/), on the gap between the configuration you designed and the one that's actually running, and why the invoice tends to notice first.

As more posts pile up, the few I'd hand a stranger will live here. Everything else is in the [archive](/blog/archive/), by year, or under [tags](/blog/tags/), by topic. If you'd rather not check back, there's an [RSS feed](/feed/).
