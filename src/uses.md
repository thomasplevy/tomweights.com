## This site

- Built with [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), and deployed by GitHub Actions whenever something lands on trunk.
- Set entirely in [IBM Plex Mono](https://www.ibm.com/plex/), because I spend most of my day in a monospace font and saw no reason to stop at the browser.
- The look is adapted from WordPress's [Twenty Twenty-Three](https://wordpress.org/themes/twentytwentythree/) theme.
- The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/thomasplevy/tomweights.com), typos and all.
