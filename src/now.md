## Writing

I'm writing again, which I didn't entirely expect after the better part of a decade off (the [About](/about/) page has the excuse). The first post back is about [the gap between the configuration you designed and the one that's actually running](/blog/intended-vs-running/).

Next is something about critical thinking now that everyone has an assistant on call. My working theory is that handing a decision to a language model doesn't make an indecisive person decisive. It gives them a more articulate way to stay undecided. I also suspect that the more summaries we generate, the less any of us actually reads. I may be wrong about both, which is most of the reason to write it.
